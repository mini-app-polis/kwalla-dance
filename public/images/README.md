Export around 1200px on the long edge. Anything larger is wasted — nothing on
this page displays an image wider than about 600 CSS pixels.

## What to shoot

**Daylight, studio, or ambient-lit social floor.** This is the actual
constraint and it is worth being explicit with Kristen about it.

The site's palette is built on a warm paper background. A dark ballroom
photo — black background, magenta and blue stage wash — sits on that page as
a hole rather than an image, and the coloured lighting fights the clay accent
in the buttons. Competition photos are often exactly this.

If the only photos she loves are stage-lit, that is fine and worth knowing
early: the palette should switch to the darker sage variant rather than the
photos being forced to work against a light page.

Beyond that: horizontal headroom on the hero image, since text sits beside it
on desktop and beneath it on mobile.
